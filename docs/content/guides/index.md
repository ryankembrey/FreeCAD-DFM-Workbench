# Guides

How to get the most out of each part of the workbench.

- [Injection molding](injection-molding.md): pull direction, side actions, nominal wall, and the
  tools for draft and undercuts.
- [3D printing](3d-printing.md): FDM, resin and powder bed printing, and what each asks for.
- [Process Library](process-library.md): change any value, criticality or message, and add your own
  processes and materials.
- [History and reports](history-and-reports.md): compare runs, and export a PDF report or a CSV
  file.
- [Contours and inspection](contours-and-inspection.md): color the model by thickness, draft,
  undercut or curvature, and check symmetry or compare two bodies.
